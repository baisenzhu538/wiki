## Reusable Knowledge

- The MUSE model is a four-layer framework for understanding AI's impact: Miracle (M), Use (U), Startup (S), and Evolution (E).
- Miracle layer focuses on AGI speculation, career transformation, productivity leaps, ethics/law, and bold sci-fi scenarios.
- Use layer emphasizes practical AI adoption: prompt engineering, agents, multimodal tools, AI programming, and cost reduction.
- Startup layer covers AI-native entrepreneurship: trend prediction, creative ideation, high-risk hypothesis testing, and low-cost validation.
- Evolution layer tracks foundational tech breakthroughs: models/algorithms, chips, robotics, cloud, edge computing, and data infrastructure.
- The framework distinguishes between "using AI to improve existing work" versus "creating new AI products/services."
- Technical capability iteration is positioned as a cross-cutting enabler spanning all layers.
- Version 3.0 of the framework was released September 5, 2025 by Yitang Lab Beijing.
